# Historical Transition Documents

Radsvinn is a **generalized, org-neutral product seed**. Its public project history begins with
this repository; source-organization transition material is intentionally not part of the
product documentation.

For what shipped in this repo and when, see the **[CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md)** at the repo
root.

For how to work in this repo today, see:

  connecting your tracker, your repositories, and your coupling map.
- [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) — components, process topology, data flow, data
  stores, and the safety spine.
- [docs/FAQ.md](FAQ.md) — the whole product explained in one screen.
- [docs/CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — the on-ramp for code contributors.
- [docs/OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md) — commands and environment for running Radsvinn.
